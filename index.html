What the real exam looks like

The Dr. Homi Bhabha Balvaidnyanik Competition is conducted by the Greater Bombay Science Teachers' Association for Standards 6 and 9. Round 1 is a written multiple-choice paper of 100 marks in 90 minutes covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology and general science awareness, with no negative marking. Students who clear it go on to a practical round and then to a project and interview. Questions follow the Maharashtra State Board science textbooks most closely, and reading the corresponding CBSE and CISCE chapters for Standards 6 to 10 fills the remaining gaps.
